THE GREEN LIGHT

AN INTERACTIVE REVERIE

The greenlight.

Across the dark water, a light still burns.
Some things are always just out of reach.

The atmosphere

Move gently. Stay a while.

A study in distance & desire

So close.
So far away.

At the end of a dock, across a dark and silent bay, a single green light holds everything Gatsby cannot reach.

Inspired by F. Scott Fitzgerald's The Great Gatsby and its 2013 film adaptation, this interactive reverie invites you to stand at the water's edge. There is nowhere to go. Nothing to do. Just a light, and the distance between.

“Gatsby believed in the green light.”
F. Scott Fitzgerald, The Great Gatsby, 1925

Rendered in real time with Three.js. Procedural water, exponential fog, and a single luminous point across the bay. Headphones recommended.
